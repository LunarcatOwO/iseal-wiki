title: Frequently Asked Questions
description: Common questions about Parkour Project and quick answers.
---

### What server software do I need?

A Paper or Folia server on **1.21.11+**, with **SealLib** installed. See
[Quick Start](/parkour/quick-start).

### Does it work on Folia?

Yes, Parkour Project runs on both Paper and Folia.

### Moves aren't working for me. Why?

Check that:

- you're in **Survival** or **Adventure** mode (moves are off in Creative and Spectator),
- your main hand is **empty** (several moves need it),
- the move is unlocked and enabled for you (`/parkour moves list`),
- the move isn't on cooldown.

### How do players get moves?

By default every move has `default_unlocked: true`, so new players start with all of them. Admins
can change this per player with `/parkour moves unlock|lock`, or per move in
[`moves.yml`](/parkour/reference/moves-yml). See [Commands](/parkour/commands).

### A player is stuck flying or moving strangely. How do I fix it?

Run `/parkour reset [player]`. It stops any move in progress and puts their gravity, flight and walk
speed back to normal.

### How do I see my cooldowns?

Turn on a cooldown display with `/parkour ui cooldowns enable <BOSSBAR|CHAT|TOOLBAR>`. You can have
more than one on at a time. `/parkour cooldowns get` also lists your active cooldowns.

### Why did my flow score reset?

Your score drains over time and resets to zero when you die. See
[Style System](/parkour/style-system).

### Can I change how many points a trick is worth?

Yes. Every bonus's `bonus` value is in [`bonuses.yml`](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml). You can also
turn bonuses off or change what they take to trigger, then run `/parkour reload`.

### How do I add my own moves or bonuses?

With the addon API. See [API & Addons](/parkour/api-usage).

### Does the plugin collect data?

It can send two kinds of **anonymous** data: usage statistics, and error reports when something goes
wrong. Both are controlled by `allow-metrics` in [`config.yml`](/parkour/configuration). Set it to
`false` to opt out of both.
