Welcome to the **Parkour Project** wiki!

Parkour Project turns any Minecraft server into a free-running playground. Players chain together
**wall runs, wall jumps, clings, vaults, slides, rolls and tic tacs** to move through the world, and
the plugin rewards smooth, creative movement with a **style system** inspired by character-action
games.

<Callout type="info">
  Parkour Project needs **SealLib** and runs on **Paper** or **Folia** **1.21.11+**.
</Callout>

## How it plays

<div className='fd-steps [&_h3]:fd-step'>

### Players get the moves
Every player starts with all eight moves unlocked. Moves use natural inputs like sprinting along a
wall, tapping sneak, or clicking a ledge. Admins can lock, unlock, enable or disable moves per player.

### Moves build a combo
Every move adds to your **combo**, which builds a multiplier of up to **8×**. Mixing different moves
builds it faster than repeating the same one.

### Stylish play triggers bonuses
Specific tricks and situations award named **bonuses** like `+NICE DROP` or `+LEAP OF FAITH`.
Bonuses are what raise your **flow score**, and each one is multiplied by your current combo, so the
same trick is worth far more mid-combo. They come in four categories of increasing difficulty.

### Score raises your flow rank
Your score maps to a **flow rank**, from **D** up to **Flow State**, shown on your scoreboard. Higher
ranks grant buffs like Speed and Jump Boost. Score drains constantly, and faster at higher ranks, so
you have to keep scoring to keep your rank.

</div>
