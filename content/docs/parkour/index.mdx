Welcome to the **Parkour Project** wiki!

Parkour Project turns any Minecraft server into a free-running playground. Players chain together
**wall runs, vaults, slides, rolls and tic-tacs** to flow through the world, and the plugin rewards
smooth, creative movement with a **style-scoring system** inspired by character-action games.

<Callout type="info">
  Parkour Project depends on **SealLib** and supports both **Paper** and **Folia** servers. It
  requires **1.21.11+**.
</Callout>

## How it works

<div className='fd-steps [&_h3]:fd-step'>

### Players unlock moves
Each ability is a **move** that can be enabled, disabled, unlocked or locked per player. Moves are
performed with natural inputs like sprinting into a wall or sneaking to roll.

### Movement builds a combo
Chaining moves keeps a **combo** alive, and the longer and more varied it gets, the higher its
multiplier climbs. Let it lapse and it resets.

### Stylish play triggers bonuses
Specific tricks and situations award named **bonuses** (like `+NICE DROP` or `+LEAP OF FAITH`).
Bonuses are the only thing that raises your **flow score**, and each one pays out its base value
multiplied by your current combo, so the same trick is worth far more mid-combo. They're grouped into
four categories of increasing difficulty.

### Score raises your flow rank
Your live score maps to a **flow rank** shown on your scoreboard, running from **D** up to
**Flow State**. Score decays constantly, and faster at higher ranks, so you have to keep scoring to
keep your rank.

</div>
