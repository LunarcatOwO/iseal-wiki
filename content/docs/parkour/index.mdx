Welcome to the **Parkour Project** wiki!

Parkour Project turns any Minecraft server into a free-running playground. Players chain together
**wall runs, vaults, slides, rolls and tic-tacs** to flow through the world — and the plugin rewards
smooth, creative movement with a **style-scoring system** inspired by character-action games.

<Callout type="info">
  Parkour Project depends on **SealLib** and supports both **Paper** and **Folia** servers. It
  requires **1.21.11+**.
</Callout>

## How it works

<div className='fd-steps [&_h3]:fd-step'>

### Players unlock moves
Each ability is a **move** that can be enabled, disabled, unlocked or locked per player. Moves are
performed with natural inputs — sprinting into a wall, sneaking to roll, and so on.

### Movement builds a combo
Chaining moves keeps a **combo** alive, and the longer and more varied it gets, the more each move
adds to it. Let it lapse and it resets.

### Stylish play triggers bonuses
Specific tricks and situations award named **bonuses** (like `+NICE DROP` or `+LEAP OF FAITH`), each
worth a burst of points. Bonuses are what actually raise your **flow score** — and a long combo is
what puts the biggest ones in reach. They're grouped into four categories of increasing difficulty.

### Score raises your flow rank
Your live score maps to a **flow rank** — from **D** up to **Flow State** (the top rank, called `MAX`
internally) — shown on your scoreboard. Score decays constantly, and faster at higher ranks, so you
have to keep scoring to keep your rank.

</div>
