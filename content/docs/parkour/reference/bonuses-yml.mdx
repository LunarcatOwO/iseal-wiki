---

`bonuses.yml` lists every style bonus. For how each one is earned in play, see the
[Style System](/parkour/style-system).

## Shared settings

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `enabled` | Whether the bonus can trigger. |
| `bonus` | Points added to your flow score when it triggers. |
| `category` | One of `FILLER`, `ADVANCED`, `COMBAT` or `MYTHIC`. |
| `display_name` | How the bonus appears on the scoreboard, in [MiniMessage](https://docs.advntr.dev/minimessage/format.html) format (gradients, `<bold>` and so on). |

Any other settings on a bonus control what it takes to trigger (time windows, distances, mob
counts). Those are explained per bonus on the [Style System](/parkour/style-system) pages.

<Callout type="info">
  In this version, `+NICE DROP` always awards 60 points and always shows as bold gold text, whatever
  its `bonus` and `display_name` are set to.
</Callout>
