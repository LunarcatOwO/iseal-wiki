## Things to know

- **Ticks:** Minecraft runs at 20 ticks per second, so a `cooldown` of `40` is 2 seconds.
- **Speeds and forces** (like `0.5`) are small on purpose. Treat them as dials: raise them for more,
  lower them for less.
- **`enabled`** turns a move or bonus on or off entirely.
- **Boss bar colors** are `PINK`, `BLUE`, `RED`, `GREEN`, `YELLOW`, `PURPLE` and `WHITE`.
- **Names** use [MiniMessage](https://docs.advntr.dev/minimessage/format.html) formatting, so tags
  like `<bold>`, `<green>` and `<gradient:#a:#b>` work.

## Applying changes

After editing `moves.yml` or `bonuses.yml`, run:

```text
/parkour reload
```

<Callout title="When to restart" type="warn">
  `/parkour reload` doesn't pick up `config.yml` or every single setting. **Restart the server** if a
  change doesn't seem to take effect.
</Callout>
