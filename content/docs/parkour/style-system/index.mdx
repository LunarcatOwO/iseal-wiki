Every stylish thing you pull off is worth points. **Bonuses** pay out, your **flow score** climbs,
and your **flow rank** goes up with it — but the score bleeds away the whole time, and it bleeds
faster the higher you are. Standing still is the only way to lose it.

## Flow score

Your flow score goes up in exactly one way: **triggering a bonus**. Each bonus adds its `bonus` value
from `bonuses.yml` straight to your score. Moves on their own are worth nothing here — they matter
because they're how you set up and land bonuses.

Meanwhile the score decays on its own. The decay rate is tied to your current rank: at **D** you lose
a trickle, at **Flow State** you're bleeding 60 points per decay tick. Reaching the top rank is one
thing, holding it is another.

## Combos

The **combo** is a separate meter, and *this* is what moves feed. Every move you perform adds combo
points and bumps the **combo multiplier**, which scales from **1× up to 8×** as the combo builds.
Combo points drain constantly and the combo dies when they run out, so you have to keep moving to
keep it.

Two things shape how much a move is worth:

- **The multiplier** — a move performed deep into a combo is worth far more than the first one.
- **Freshness** — repeating a move you've just used is worth less than switching it up. Varied
  movement builds the combo faster.

The combo doesn't feed your score directly, but several bonuses are gated on it — **+STILL ALIVE**
wants a 10+ move combo, for instance — so a long combo is what puts the big payouts in reach.

## Flow ranks

Your score maps to a **flow rank**, shown on your scoreboard with its own animated gradient. Ranks
climb as your score rises and drop back as it decays.

| Rank | On the scoreboard | Score needed | Decay per tick |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| **D** | ᴅᴜʟʟ | 0 | 1 |
| **C** | ᴄʜɪʟʟ | 500 | 2 |
| **B** | ʙʀɪsᴋ | 1,200 | 4 |
| **A** | ᴀɢɪʟᴇ | 2,500 | 8 |
| **S** | sᴡɪғᴛ | 4,500 | 15 |
| **SS** | ssᴜᴘᴇʀsᴏɴɪᴄ | 7,000 | 25 |
| **SSS** | sssʏɴᴄʜʀᴏɴɪᴢᴇᴅ | 10,000 | 40 |
| **Flow State** | ғʟᴏᴡ sᴛᴀᴛᴇ | 14,000 | 60 |

Rank isn't only cosmetic: **+HIGH PROFILE** only fires at **S** or above, and **+FLOW STATE** pays
out for holding the top rank. `Flow State` is called `MAX` in code and in `minimum_rank` settings.

## Bonuses

A bonus is a named trick — `+NICE DROP`, `+LEAP OF FAITH` — worth a burst of points and a callout on
screen. Every one lives in `bonuses.yml` where you can change its value, switch it off, or retune
what it takes to trigger.

They're split into four categories of rising difficulty:
