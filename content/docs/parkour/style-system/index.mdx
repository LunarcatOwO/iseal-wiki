Every stylish thing you pull off is worth points. **Bonuses** pay out, your **flow score** climbs,
and your **flow rank** goes up with it. The catch is that the score bleeds away the whole time, and
it bleeds faster the higher you are. Standing still is the only way to lose it.

## Flow score

Your flow score goes up in exactly one way: **triggering a bonus**. Nothing else adds to it, and
moves on their own are worth nothing here. What a bonus pays out is its `bonus` value from
`bonuses.yml` **multiplied by your current combo multiplier**, so the same trick can be worth eight
times as much depending on the combo you land it in.

Meanwhile the score decays on its own. The decay rate is tied to your current rank: at **D** you lose
a trickle, at **Flow State** you're bleeding 60 points per decay tick. Reaching the top rank is one
thing, holding it is another.

## Combos

The combo is the meter that moves feed, and it's what turns a bonus from small into huge. Every move
you perform adds combo points and pushes up the **combo multiplier**, which scales from **1× up to
8×**. When a bonus fires, its configured value is multiplied by whatever that multiplier currently
sits at:

> A 100-point bonus landed cold is worth **100**. The same bonus landed at the top of a combo is
> worth **800**.

That's why chasing high-value bonuses on their own doesn't work. Building the combo first is what
makes them pay.

Combo points drain constantly and the combo dies when they run out, so you have to keep moving to
hold the multiplier up. Two things decide how much a move contributes:

- **The multiplier itself.** A move performed deep into a combo adds far more than the first one, so
  the meter accelerates as it grows.
- **Freshness.** Repeating a move you've just used adds less than switching it up. Varied movement
  builds the combo faster.

On top of the multiplier, the combo also **gates** some bonuses outright: **+STILL ALIVE** won't fire
at all without a 10+ move combo behind it.

## Flow ranks

Your score maps to a **flow rank**, shown on your scoreboard with its own animated gradient. Ranks
climb as your score rises and drop back as it decays.

| Rank | On the scoreboard | Score needed | Decay per tick |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| **D** | ᴅᴜʟʟ | 0 | 1 |
| **C** | ᴄʜɪʟʟ | 500 | 2 |
| **B** | ʙʀɪsᴋ | 1,200 | 4 |
| **A** | ᴀɢɪʟᴇ | 2,500 | 8 |
| **S** | sᴡɪғᴛ | 4,500 | 15 |
| **SS** | ssᴜᴘᴇʀsᴏɴɪᴄ | 7,000 | 25 |
| **SSS** | sssʏɴᴄʜʀᴏɴɪᴢᴇᴅ | 10,000 | 40 |
| **Flow State** | ғʟᴏᴡ sᴛᴀᴛᴇ | 14,000 | 60 |

Rank isn't only cosmetic: **+HIGH PROFILE** only fires at **S** or above, and **+FLOW STATE** pays
out for holding the top rank. In config, Flow State is written as `max`, which is what you'd put in a
`minimum_rank` setting to require it.

## Bonuses

A bonus is a named trick like `+NICE DROP` or `+LEAP OF FAITH`, worth a burst of points and a callout
on screen. Every one lives in `bonuses.yml` where you can change its value, switch it off, or retune
what it takes to trigger.

They're split into four categories of rising difficulty:

  <Card title="Mythic" href="/parkour/style-system/mythic" description="Frame-perfect timing and near-death landings, paid out accordingly." />

<Callout title="Everything here is a default" type="warn">
  The point values on these pages are the **base values** from a stock `bonuses.yml`, before your

  retune their triggers, so what your server awards may differ. See the
