title: Style System
description: Flow score, combos, flow ranks and their perks, and the four categories of style bonuses.
---

Every stylish thing you pull off is worth points. **Bonuses** pay out, your **flow score** climbs,
and your **flow rank** goes up with it. The catch is that your score drains the whole time, and it
drains faster the higher you are, so you have to keep moving to stay on top.

## Flow score

Your flow score goes up in one way: **triggering a bonus**. Each bonus adds its points (its `bonus`
value in `bonuses.yml`) to your score. Moves on their own don't add score.

Your score also drains on its own, and the higher your rank, the faster it goes: a trickle at **D**,
60 points per tick at **Flow State**. Reaching the top rank is one thing; holding it is another.

**Dying** resets your score and your combo to zero.

## Your scoreboard

As soon as you have any flow score, a scoreboard appears on the side of your screen showing:

- your current **rank**,
- a **progress bar** toward the next rank,
- your **last five bonuses**,
- your **combo multiplier**.

Your flow score also shows **under your name** for other players. When your score drains back to
zero, the scoreboard disappears.

## Combos

Every move you perform adds to your **combo** and pushes up its **multiplier**, which goes from
**1× up to 8×**. Two things decide how much a move adds:

- **The multiplier itself.** A move made deep into a combo adds more than the first one, so the meter
  speeds up as it grows.
- **Freshness.** Repeating a move you just used adds less than switching it up. Varied movement
  builds the combo faster.

The combo drains constantly and ends when it runs out, so you have to keep moving to hold it up. It
also ends if your flow score drops to zero. Some bonuses need a combo going: **+CHECKPOINT** only
pays out mid-combo, and **+STILL ALIVE** needs a combo of 10 or more moves.

gradient. Ranks go up as your score rises and drop back as it drains.

### Rank perks

While you hold a rank you get its potion effects, plus particles around you that get flashier the
higher you go. A sound plays whenever your rank changes.

| Rank | Effects |
| --- | --- |
| **D** | None |
| **C** | Speed I |
| **B** | Speed I, Jump Boost I |
| **A** | Speed II, Jump Boost I |
| **S** | Speed II, Jump Boost II, Regeneration I |
| **SS** | Speed III, Jump Boost II, Regeneration I |
| **SSS** | Speed III, Jump Boost III, Regeneration II, Fire Resistance |
| **Flow State** | Speed III, Jump Boost III, Regeneration II, Fire Resistance, Glowing |

Rank also matters for some bonuses: **+HIGH PROFILE** only fires at **S** or above, and
**+FLOW STATE** pays out for holding the top rank. In config files, Flow State is written as `max`.

## Bonuses

A bonus is a named trick like `+NICE DROP` or `+LEAP OF FAITH`. It adds points to your score and
shows up on your scoreboard. Every bonus is listed in `bonuses.yml`, where you can change its points,
turn it off, or change what it takes to trigger.

They're split into four categories of rising difficulty:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Basic" href="/parkour/style-system/filler" description="Small, frequent payouts that keep you ahead of the drain between bigger tricks." />
  <Card title="Advanced Movement" href="/parkour/style-system/advanced" description="Precise routing, high-speed lines and clean timing chains." />
  <Card title="Combat Hybrid" href="/parkour/style-system/combat" description="Fighting without ever stopping your run." />
  <Card title="Mythic" href="/parkour/style-system/mythic" description="Perfect timing and near-death landings, paid out accordingly." />
</Cards>

Advanced, Combat and Mythic bonuses also play a burst of particles and a sound when you earn them
(server owners can turn this off with `external-effects` in [`config.yml`](/parkour/configuration)).

<Callout title="These are the defaults" type="warn">
  The points on these pages come from a fresh `bonuses.yml`. Server owners can change any value, turn
  off individual bonuses, or change their triggers, so your server may award different amounts. See
  the [`bonuses.yml` reference](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml).
</Callout>
