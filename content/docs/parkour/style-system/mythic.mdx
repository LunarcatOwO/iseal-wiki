**Category:** `MYTHIC` · **Design name:** "Mythic & Clutch"

The highest tier. These bonuses require **incredible mechanical precision, absolute timing, or
near-death scenarios** — and they pay out accordingly, from 800 points up to a staggering 1,500.

| Bonus | Points | How to earn | Config key |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `+CLOSE CALL` | 800 | Roll from a height that would otherwise have killed you. | `close_call` |
| `+BUT IT REFUSED` | 900 | Survive on half a heart after any roll. | `but_it_refused` |
| `+PARRY THE EARTH` | 1000 | Land a perfect (0-tick) roll. | `parry_the_earth` |
| `+FLOW STATE` | 1000 | Hold the top flow rank (**Flow State**) for more than 30 seconds. | `flow_state` |
| `+STILL ALIVE` | 1100 | End a 10+ move combo with a perfect roll. | `still_alive` |
| `+NEWTON` | 1200 | Spend 10 full seconds in the air while performing at least 5 moves. | `newton` |
| `+DEATH DEFIED` | 1200 | Achieve a 90% roll reduction with only half a heart to spare. | `death_defied` |
| `+SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY` | 1250 | Wall run and slide through a deep dark biome without setting off a single sculk sensor. | `suspicious_activity` |
| `+LEAP OF FAITH` | 1300 | Wall jump into a fall of more than 30 blocks, then land without taking any damage. | `leap_of_faith` |
| `+RECONSTRUCT WHAT?` | 1500 | Land a perfect roll cancelling over 100 points of potential damage. | `reconstruct_what` |

## Key tunables

| Config key | Field | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `but_it_refused` | `health_threshold` | `1.0` |
| `flow_state` | `required_ticks` | `600` |
| `still_alive` | `required_move_count` | `10` |
| `newton` | `required_airborne_ticks` / `required_move_count` | `200` / `5` |
| `death_defied` | `required_reduction` / `health_threshold` | `0.9` / `1.0` |
| `suspicious_activity` | `min_traversal_distance` / `sensor_trigger_radius` | `20.0` / `16.0` |
| `leap_of_faith` | `min_fall_distance` / `max_track_ticks` | `30.0` / `400` |
| `reconstruct_what` | `min_potential_damage` | `100.0` |

<Callout title="Suspicious Activity" type="warn">
  In the shipped `bonuses.yml`, `suspicious_activity` only lists its two thresholds and is missing the
  `enabled` / `bonus` / `category` / `display_name` fields present on every other bonus. If it doesn't
  fire on your server, add those fields (category `MYTHIC`, bonus `1250`) to enable it.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  `close_call` and `parry_the_earth` have no extra thresholds — they trigger purely on the feat
  itself. Full field list in the [`bonuses.yml` reference](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml).
</Callout>
