---
title: Mythic
description: The MYTHIC bonuses. Perfect timing and near-death landings, paid out accordingly.
---

The top tier, and the only bonuses worth four figures. Almost all of them run through the
[roll](/parkour/moves/roll), because surviving something that should have killed you, on the exact
tick, is the most valuable thing you can do.

They use `category: MYTHIC` in [`bonuses.yml`](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml). Each table below
shows the settings under that bonus's key.

## `+CLOSE CALL` (800 pts)

[Roll](/parkour/moves/roll) out of a fall that would otherwise have killed you. `close_call` has no
extra settings.

## `+BUT IT REFUSED` (900 pts)

Come out of any roll, perfect or not, with almost no health left.

| `but_it_refused` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `health_threshold` | `1.0` | The most health you can have left. `1.0` is half a heart. |

## `+PARRY THE EARTH` (1000 pts)

Land a perfect roll, pressing sneak on the very last tick before you hit the ground.
`parry_the_earth` has no extra settings.

## `+FLOW STATE` (1000 pts)

Hold the top [flow rank](/parkour/style-system) for a long time.

| `flow_state` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_ticks` | `600` | How long you have to stay at Flow State, in ticks (30 seconds). |

## `+STILL ALIVE` (1100 pts)

Finish a long combo with a perfect roll.

| `still_alive` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_move_count` | `10` | How many moves the combo needs before the roll. |

## `+NEWTON` (1200 pts)

Stay in the air for a long time while chaining moves the whole way.

| `newton` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_airborne_ticks` | `200` | How long you have to stay off the ground, in ticks (10 seconds). |
| `required_move_count` | `5` | How many moves you have to use while up there. |

## `+DEATH DEFIED` (1200 pts)

Land a roll with the full 90% damage reduction and come out of it barely alive.

| `death_defied` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_reduction` | `0.9` | How much of the fall damage the roll has to cancel. `0.9` is the most a roll can do, so this asks for a perfect one. |
| `health_threshold` | `1.0` | The most health you can have left. `1.0` is half a heart. |

## `+SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY` (1250 pts)

[Wall run](/parkour/moves/wall-run) and [slide](/parkour/moves/slide) your way through a Deep Dark
biome without setting off a single sculk sensor.

| `suspicious_activity` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_traversal_distance` | `20.0` | How far you have to travel through the biome, in blocks. |
| `sensor_trigger_radius` | `16.0` | How far away a sculk sensor can be and still count, in blocks. |

## `+LEAP OF FAITH` (1300 pts)

[Wall jump](/parkour/moves/wall-jump) into an enormous fall and land without taking any damage.

| `leap_of_faith` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_fall_distance` | `30.0` | How far you have to fall, in blocks. |
| `max_track_ticks` | `400` | How long the fall can last before it stops counting, in ticks (20 seconds). |

## `+RECONSTRUCT WHAT?` (1500 pts)

Land a perfect roll from a fall that would have dealt an enormous amount of damage.

| `reconstruct_what` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_potential_damage` | `100.0` | How much damage the fall would have dealt without the roll. |
