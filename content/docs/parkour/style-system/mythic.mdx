---
title: Mythic
description: The MYTHIC bonuses — frame-perfect timing and near-death landings, paid out accordingly.
---

The top tier, and the only bonuses worth four figures. Almost all of them run through the
[roll](/parkour/moves/roll): surviving something that should have killed you, on the exact tick, is
the most valuable thing you can do.

They use `category: MYTHIC` in [`bonuses.yml`](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml).

## `+CLOSE CALL` — 800 pts

`close_call` — [roll](/parkour/moves/roll) out of a fall that would otherwise have killed you. No
thresholds; it fires on the survival itself.

## `+BUT IT REFUSED` — 900 pts

`but_it_refused` — survive on a sliver of health after any roll.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `health_threshold` | `1.0` | How much health you can be left with, in half-hearts. |

## `+PARRY THE EARTH` — 1000 pts

`parry_the_earth` — land a 0-tick roll. No thresholds; it fires on the perfect timing itself.

## `+FLOW STATE` — 1000 pts

`flow_state` — hold the top [flow rank](/parkour/style-system) without letting it decay.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_ticks` | `600` | How long you have to stay at Flow State. |

## `+STILL ALIVE` — 1100 pts

`still_alive` — finish a long combo with a perfect roll.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_move_count` | `10` | How many moves the combo needs before the roll. |

## `+NEWTON` — 1200 pts

`newton` — stay airborne for a long stretch while chaining moves the whole way.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_airborne_ticks` | `200` | How long you have to stay off the ground. |
| `required_move_count` | `5` | How many moves you have to perform up there. |

## `+DEATH DEFIED` — 1200 pts

`death_defied` — pull off a near-maximum roll reduction and come out of it barely alive.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_reduction` | `0.9` | The fraction of the fall damage the roll has to cancel. `0.9` is the most a roll can do, so this asks for a perfect one. |
| `health_threshold` | `1.0` | How much health you can be left with, in half-hearts. |

## `+SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY` — 1250 pts

`suspicious_activity` — [wall run](/parkour/moves/wall-run) and [slide](/parkour/moves/slide) your
way through a deep dark biome without setting off a single sculk sensor.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_traversal_distance` | `20.0` | How far you have to travel through the biome, in blocks. |
| `sensor_trigger_radius` | `16.0` | How far around you sculk sensors are watched for. |

## `+LEAP OF FAITH` — 1300 pts

`leap_of_faith` — [wall jump](/parkour/moves/wall-jump) into an enormous fall and land without taking
a scratch.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_fall_distance` | `30.0` | How far you have to fall, in blocks. |
| `max_track_ticks` | `400` | How long the fall is tracked before it stops counting. |

## `+RECONSTRUCT WHAT?` — 1500 pts

`reconstruct_what` — land a perfect roll against an enormous amount of incoming fall damage.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_potential_damage` | `100.0` | How much damage the fall had to be worth before the roll. |
