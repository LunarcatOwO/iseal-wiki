---
title: Basic
description: The FILLER bonuses. Small, frequent payouts that keep you ahead of the score drain.
---

These are the bonuses you'll hit over and over. Each one is worth little, but they fire often
enough to keep you ahead of the score drain between bigger tricks, and most of them set up those
bigger tricks anyway.

They use `category: FILLER` in [`bonuses.yml`](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml). Each table below
shows the settings under that bonus's key. Points are the base value before your
[combo multiplier](/parkour/style-system#combos) is applied.

## `+KEEP UP` (30 pts)

Chain any two *different* moves together quickly.

| `keep_up` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_streak_time_millis` | `1300` | How long you have between the two moves, in milliseconds. |

## `+NICE DROP` (60 pts)

[Roll](/parkour/moves/roll) out of a fall of a few blocks or more.

| `nice_drop` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_blocks_fallen` | `4` | How far you have to fall, in blocks. |

## `+SOFT LANDING` (75 pts)

Land any [roll](/parkour/moves/roll) from a fall that wouldn't have killed you. `soft_landing` has
no extra settings.

## `+CHECKPOINT` (100 pts)

[Wall cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling) briefly to catch your breath in the middle of a combo, then
carry on.

| `checkpoint` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_cling_time_millis` | `1000` | The longest cling that still counts as a pause, in milliseconds. |

## `+SMOOTH TRANSITION` (100 pts)

Drop straight into a [slide](/parkour/moves/slide) coming out of a
[climb vault](/parkour/moves/climb-vault).

| `smooth_transition` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_cling_time_millis` | `1000` | How long after the vault the slide still counts, in milliseconds. |

## `+TOUCH AND GO` (100 pts)

Grab a wall with a [cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling) and [wall jump](/parkour/moves/wall-jump) off
it almost immediately.

| `touch_and_go` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_cling_time_millis` | `500` | The longest cling that still counts as a touch, in milliseconds. |

## `+MOMENTUM CONSERVATION` (100 pts)

Come out of a [quick vault](/parkour/moves/quick-vault) at full speed, without clipping the obstacle.

| `momentum_conservation` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `speed_match_threshold` | `1.2` | How fast you have to be going after the vault compared to before it. Above `1.0` means you have to come out *faster*. |
| `delay_ticks` | `10` | How long after the vault your speed is checked, in ticks. |

## `+WALL TO WALL` (120 pts)

Cling to one wall, then cling to a different one without touching the ground in between.

| `wall_to_wall` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_chain_time_millis` | `10000` | How long you have between the two clings, in milliseconds. |

## `+STAYING AIRBORNE` (150 pts)

Stay off the ground for a while, using any moves you like.

| `staying_airborne` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_airborne_ticks` | `60` | How long you have to stay in the air, in ticks (3 seconds). |

## `+WAVEDASH` (150 pts)

Jump out of a [slide](/parkour/moves/slide) the instant it starts.

| `wavedash` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_chain_time_millis` | `50` | How soon after the slide starts you have to jump, in milliseconds. |
| `min_y` | `0.2` | How much upward speed the jump needs. |

## `+CONVEYOR BELT` (150 pts)

Hold one unbroken slide down a slope.

| `conveyor_belt` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_downhill_distance` | `5.0` | How far downhill the slide has to carry you, in blocks. |
