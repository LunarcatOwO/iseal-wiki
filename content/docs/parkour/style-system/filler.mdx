**Category:** `FILLER` · **Design name:** "Bread & Butter"

These are the bread-and-butter bonuses: low-value, highly repeatable actions you weave between bigger
tricks to **freeze your score decay** and keep a combo alive. Individually small, they add up fast.

| Bonus | Points | How to earn | Config key |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `+KEEP UP` | 30 | Chain any two different moves together within the streak window (~1.3s). | `keep_up` |
| `+NICE DROP` | 60 | Fall 4+ blocks and transition straight into a sprint without losing speed. | `nice_drop` |
| `+SOFT LANDING` | 75 | Land any successful roll from a non-lethal height. | `soft_landing` |
| `+CHECKPOINT` | 100 | Wall cling to rest for ≤1 second, then continue the combo. | `checkpoint` |
| `+SMOOTH TRANSITION` | 100 | Slide immediately out of a climb vault. | `smooth_transition` |
| `+TOUCH AND GO` | 100 | Touch a wall with a cling for under half a second before leaping off. | `touch_and_go` |
| `+MOMENTUM CONSERVATION` | 100 | Get the perfect speed boost on a quick vault. | `momentum_conservation` |
| `+WALL TO WALL` | 120 | Cling on one wall directly into a cling on another wall. | `wall_to_wall` |
| `+STAYING AIRBORNE` | 150 | Spend ~3 seconds off the ground using any combination of moves. | `staying_airborne` |
| `+WAVEDASH` | 150 | End a slide the instant after starting it (jump-cancel). | `wavedash` |
| `+CONVEYOR BELT` | 150 | Maintain a single continuous slide for more than 5 blocks down an incline. | `conveyor_belt` |

## Key tunables

| Config key | Field | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `keep_up` | `max_streak_time_millis` | `1300` |
| `nice_drop` | `min_blocks_fallen` | `4` |
| `checkpoint` | `max_cling_time_millis` | `1000` |
| `smooth_transition` | `max_cling_time_millis` | `1000` |
| `touch_and_go` | `max_cling_time_millis` | `500` |
| `momentum_conservation` | `speed_match_threshold` / `delay_ticks` | `1.2` / `10` |
| `wall_to_wall` | `max_chain_time_millis` | `10000` |
| `staying_airborne` | `required_airborne_ticks` | `60` |
| `wavedash` | `max_chain_time_millis` / `min_y` | `50` / `0.2` |
| `conveyor_belt` | `min_downhill_distance` | `5.0` |
