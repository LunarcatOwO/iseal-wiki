---
title: Combat Hybrid
description: The COMBAT bonuses. Fighting without ever dropping out of your run.
---

Combat bonuses only pay out if you engage, evade or kill *while still moving*. Stopping to fight
scores nothing; the whole category exists to make a fight part of the route instead of a break in it.

They use `category: COMBAT` in [`bonuses.yml`](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml). Each table below is
the block you'll find under that bonus's key. Points are the base value before your
[combo multiplier](/parkour/style-system) is applied.

## `+CLOSE QUARTERS` (300 pts)

[Wall jump](/parkour/moves/wall-jump) off a surface right next to a hostile mob.

| `close_quarters` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mob_radius` | `2.0` | How close a hostile mob has to be to the wall, in blocks. |

## `+SWEEP THE LEG` (350 pts)

Knock back or damage a mob by [sliding](/parkour/moves/slide) straight into it. `sweep_the_leg` has
no thresholds; it fires on the tackle itself.

## `+DISRESPECT` (400 pts)

Punch a mob with an empty hand while sliding or clinging.

| `disrespect` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tackle_guard_window_ticks` | `4` | Hits landed this soon after a slide tackle are ignored, so the tackle itself can't count as the punch. |

## `+EMERGENCY MEETING` (400 pts)

Drop out of a [wall cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling) or a [roll](/parkour/moves/roll) into the
middle of a pack of mobs.

| `emergency_meeting` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mob_radius` | `3.0` | How far around your landing point mobs are counted, in blocks. |
| `min_mobs` | `3` | How many have to be in that radius. |

## `+CRITICAL DROP` (400 pts)

Land a critical melee hit right after releasing a [wall cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling).

| `critical_drop` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `window_ticks` | `20` | How long after letting go of the wall the hit still counts. |

## `+SPLATTERED` (450 pts)

Crush a mob with a [roll](/parkour/moves/roll). `splattered` has no thresholds; it fires on the crush
itself.

## `+BIG SHOT` (500 pts)

Land a bow shot while [sliding](/parkour/moves/slide). `big_shot` has no thresholds; it fires on the
hit itself.

## `+HIT & RUN` (500 pts)

Kill an enemy with a [tic tac](/parkour/moves/tic-tac).

| `hit_and_run` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `window_ticks` | `20` | How long after the tic tac the kill still counts. |

## `+FROM ABOVE` (500 pts)

Kill an enemy while dropping out of a [climb vault](/parkour/moves/climb-vault).

| `from_above` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `window_ticks` | `20` | How long after the vault the kill still counts. |

## `+FLYBY` (600 pts)

Kill something mid [wall run](/parkour/moves/wall-run). `flyby` has no thresholds; it fires on the
kill itself.

## `+BLUE HEART` (650 pts)

[Tic tac](/parkour/moves/tic-tac) to reverse your horizontal direction and dodge an incoming
projectile.

| `blue_heart` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `opposite_direction_threshold` | `-0.3` | How sharp the reversal has to be, as a dot product of your direction before and after. More negative means a harder turn is required. |

## `+NEO` (700 pts)

Dodge or slide under several projectiles in quick succession.

| `neo` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_dodges` | `3` | How many projectiles you have to evade. |
| `window_millis` | `10000` | How long you have to evade them all. |
| `slide_poll_interval_ticks` | `4` | How often a slide is checked against nearby projectiles. |
