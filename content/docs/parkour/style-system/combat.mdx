---
title: Combat Hybrid
description: The COMBAT bonuses. Fighting without ever stopping your run.
---

Combat bonuses only pay out if you fight, dodge or kill *while still moving*. Stopping to fight
scores nothing; the whole category is about making a fight part of the route instead of a break in
it. Only **hostile mobs** count.

They use `category: COMBAT` in [`bonuses.yml`](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml). Each table below
shows the settings under that bonus's key.

## `+CLOSE QUARTERS` (300 pts)

[Wall jump](/parkour/moves/wall-jump) off a surface right next to a hostile mob.

| `close_quarters` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mob_radius` | `2.0` | How close the mob has to be, in blocks. |

## `+SWEEP THE LEG` (350 pts)

Hit a mob by [sliding](/parkour/moves/slide) into it. `sweep_the_leg` has no extra settings.

## `+DISRESPECT` (400 pts)

Punch a mob with an empty hand while sliding or clinging.

| `disrespect` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tackle_guard_window_ticks` | `4` | Punches this soon after a slide tackle don't count, so the tackle itself isn't mistaken for a punch. |

## `+EMERGENCY MEETING` (400 pts)

Drop out of a [wall cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling), or land a [roll](/parkour/moves/roll), right
in the middle of a group of mobs.

| `emergency_meeting` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mob_radius` | `3.0` | How far around your landing spot mobs are counted, in blocks. |
| `min_mobs` | `3` | How many mobs have to be there. |

## `+CRITICAL DROP` (400 pts)

Land a critical hit right after letting go of a [wall cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling).

| `critical_drop` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `window_ticks` | `20` | How long after letting go the hit still counts, in ticks. |

## `+SPLATTERED` (450 pts)

Crush a mob by landing a [roll](/parkour/moves/roll) on it. `splattered` has no extra settings.

## `+BIG SHOT` (500 pts)

Fire a bow while [sliding](/parkour/moves/slide). `big_shot` has no extra settings.

## `+HIT & RUN` (500 pts)

Kill a mob by [tic tacking](/parkour/moves/tic-tac) off it.

| `hit_and_run` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `window_ticks` | `20` | How long after the tic tac the kill still counts, in ticks. |

## `+FROM ABOVE` (500 pts)

Kill a mob while you're still in the air after a [climb vault](/parkour/moves/climb-vault).

| `from_above` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `window_ticks` | `20` | How long after the vault the kill still counts, in ticks. |

## `+FLYBY` (600 pts)

Kill a mob in the middle of a [wall run](/parkour/moves/wall-run). `flyby` has no extra settings.

## `+BLUE HEART` (650 pts)

[Tic tac](/parkour/moves/tic-tac) (off a wall or a mob) to reverse direction and dodge an incoming
projectile.

| `blue_heart` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `opposite_direction_threshold` | `-0.3` | How sharply you have to turn. Lower (more negative) values need a harder turn; `-1` is a full reversal. |

## `+NEO` (700 pts)

Dodge several projectiles in a short time, either by sliding under them or by getting out of the way
in mid-air with a tic tac or wall jump.

| `neo` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_dodges` | `3` | How many projectiles you have to dodge. |
| `window_millis` | `10000` | How long you have to dodge them all, in milliseconds. |
| `slide_poll_interval_ticks` | `4` | How often (in ticks) a slide checks for projectiles passing over you. |
