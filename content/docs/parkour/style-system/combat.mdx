**Category:** `COMBAT` · **Design name:** "Combat Hybrid"

Combat bonuses are style variants that require you to **actively engage, evade, or eliminate enemies
while maintaining motion**. Fighting isn't a pause in your run — it's part of the flow.

| Bonus | Points | How to earn | Config key |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `+CLOSE QUARTERS` | 300 | Wall jump off a surface within 2 blocks of a hostile mob. | `close_quarters` |
| `+SWEEP THE LEG` | 350 | Knock back or damage a mob by sliding directly into it. | `sweep_the_leg` |
| `+DISRESPECT` | 400 | Punch a mob with an empty hand while sliding or clinging. | `disrespect` |
| `+EMERGENCY MEETING` | 400 | Drop from a wall cling or roll into the middle of a group of mobs. | `emergency_meeting` |
| `+CRITICAL DROP` | 400 | Land a critical melee hit immediately after releasing a wall cling. | `critical_drop` |
| `+SPLATTERED` | 450 | Crush a mob with a roll. | `splattered` |
| `+BIG SHOT` | 500 | Land a bow shot while sliding. | `big_shot` |
| `+HIT & RUN` | 500 | Kill an enemy with a tic tac. | `hit_and_run` |
| `+FROM ABOVE` | 500 | Kill an enemy while dropping out of a climb vault. | `from_above` |
| `+FLYBY` | 600 | Kill something while in a wall run. | `flyby` |
| `+BLUE HEART` | 650 | Instantly change horizontal direction with a tic tac to dodge an incoming projectile. | `blue_heart` |
| `+NEO` | 700 | Dodge or slide under 3 projectiles within 10 seconds. | `neo` |

## Key tunables

| Config key | Field | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `close_quarters` | `mob_radius` | `2.0` |
| `disrespect` | `tackle_guard_window_ticks` | `4` |
| `emergency_meeting` | `mob_radius` / `min_mobs` | `3.0` / `3` |
| `critical_drop` | `window_ticks` | `20` |
| `hit_and_run` | `window_ticks` | `20` |
| `from_above` | `window_ticks` | `20` |
| `blue_heart` | `opposite_direction_threshold` | `-0.3` |
| `neo` | `window_millis` / `required_dodges` / `slide_poll_interval_ticks` | `10000` / `3` / `4` |

<Callout type="info">
  `sweep_the_leg`, `splattered` and `big_shot` have no extra thresholds — they fire on the action
  itself. See the [`bonuses.yml` reference](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml) for every field.
</Callout>
