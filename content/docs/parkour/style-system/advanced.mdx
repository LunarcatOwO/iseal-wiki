---
title: Advanced Movement
description: The ADVANCED bonuses — precise routing, high-speed lines, and clean timing chains.
---

Advanced bonuses ask for deliberate routing rather than repetition: specific moves in a specific
order, held speeds, and timing windows you have to actually hit. These are what separate a competent
runner from a stylish one.

They use `category: ADVANCED` in [`bonuses.yml`](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml).

## `+FICSIT APPROVED` — 250 pts

`ficsit_approved` — land a run of perfectly-timed [quick vaults](/parkour/moves/quick-vault) or
slides across identical block heights, back-to-back.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_streak` | `3` | How many matched vaults in a row you need. |
| `max_time_millis` | `5000` | How long the whole streak can take. |

## `+CATAPULT` — 250 pts

`catapult` — [quick vault](/parkour/moves/quick-vault) directly out of a
[climb vault](/parkour/moves/climb-vault).

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_chain_time_millis` | `1200` | The gap allowed between the two vaults. |

## `+OVER THE RAILS` — 300 pts

`over_the_rails` — land a perfect [roll](/parkour/moves/roll) immediately after vaulting a fence.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_chain_time_millis` | `5000` | How long after the vault the roll still counts. |
| `allow_extra_moves` | `false` | Whether other moves may happen between the vault and the roll. |

## `+BREACH` — 300 pts

`breach` — smash through a door, trapdoor or gate with a [slide](/parkour/moves/slide). No
thresholds; it fires on the breach itself.

## `+HIGH PROFILE` — 300 pts

`high_profile` — pull off a loud or explosive move, like breaking a door, while at a high enough
flow rank.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `minimum_rank` | `s` | The lowest [flow rank](/parkour/style-system) that can trigger it. Use `max` for Flow State. |

## `+DEALMAKER` — 350 pts

`dealmaker` — land a [roll](/parkour/moves/roll) on top of a villager's workstation or a loot
container.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `workstation_materials` | list | Blocks that count as a workstation. Defaults cover the villager job blocks — see the [full file](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml). |
| `loot_materials` | list | Blocks that count as loot. Defaults are `CHEST`, `TRAPPED_CHEST` and `BARREL`. |

## `+REBOUND CLING` — 350 pts

`rebound_cling` — [tic tac](/parkour/moves/tic-tac) into a [wall cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling)
without ever touching the ground.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_window_ticks` | `40` | How long after the tic tac the cling still counts. |

## `+EFFICIENCY BUMP` — 350 pts

`efficiency_bump` — get the perfect speed boost on two consecutive
[quick vaults](/parkour/moves/quick-vault).

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `speed_match_threshold` | `1.1` | How much speed you must carry out of each vault relative to what you went in with. |
| `delay_ticks` | `2` | How long after each vault your speed is measured. |

## `+LONG WAY DOWN` — 400 pts

`long_way_down` — hold one continuous [wall run](/parkour/moves/wall-run) over a long stretch of
wall.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_wallrun_distance` | `12.0` | How far the single wall run has to carry you, in blocks. |

## `+VENTED` — 400 pts

`vented` — pass through an iron trapdoor on a full-speed [slide](/parkour/moves/slide).

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_speed` | `0.32` | The slide speed you need at the moment you pass through. |

## `+DOUBLE DOWN` — 400 pts

`double_down` — land two perfectly-timed [rolls](/parkour/moves/roll) close together.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_window_millis` | `10000` | How far apart the two rolls can be. |

## `+DEALER'S CHOICE` — 450 pts

`dealers_choice` — [slide](/parkour/moves/slide) into [wall run](/parkour/moves/wall-run) into
[tic tac](/parkour/moves/tic-tac).

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_chain_time_millis` | `3000` | How long the whole three-move chain can take. |

## `+GHOST RUN` — 500 pts

`ghost_run` — string together a long sequence of moves without taking any damage.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_moves` | `30` | How many moves the damage-free run needs. |

## `+SKYLINE` — 500 pts

`skyline` — chain [wall runs](/parkour/moves/wall-run) together without touching the ground or using
any move other than a [wall jump](/parkour/moves/wall-jump) between them.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_streak` | `3` | How many wall runs you need in the chain. |

## `+CHAOS, CHAOS!` — 600 pts

`chaos_chaos` — trigger several *distinct* moves in quick succession.

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_distinct_moves` | `5` | How many different moves you need. Repeats don't count. |
| `window_millis` | `5000` | How long you have to fit them all in. |
