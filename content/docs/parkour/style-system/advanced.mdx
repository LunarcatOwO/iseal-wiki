---
title: Advanced Movement
description: The ADVANCED bonuses. Precise routing, high-speed lines, and clean timing chains.
---

Advanced bonuses ask for deliberate routing rather than repetition: specific moves in a specific
order, held speeds, and timing windows you have to actually hit. These are what separate a competent
runner from a stylish one.

They use `category: ADVANCED` in [`bonuses.yml`](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml). Each table below is
the block you'll find under that bonus's key. Points are the base value before your
[combo multiplier](/parkour/style-system) is applied.

## `+FICSIT APPROVED` (250 pts)

Land a run of perfectly-timed [quick vaults](/parkour/moves/quick-vault) or slides across identical
block heights, back-to-back.

| `ficsit_approved` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_streak` | `3` | How many matched vaults in a row you need. |
| `max_time_millis` | `5000` | How long the whole streak can take. |

## `+CATAPULT` (250 pts)

[Quick vault](/parkour/moves/quick-vault) directly out of a
[climb vault](/parkour/moves/climb-vault).

| `catapult` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_chain_time_millis` | `1200` | The gap allowed between the two vaults. |

## `+OVER THE RAILS` (300 pts)

Land a perfect [roll](/parkour/moves/roll) immediately after vaulting a fence.

| `over_the_rails` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_chain_time_millis` | `5000` | How long after the vault the roll still counts. |
| `allow_extra_moves` | `false` | Whether other moves may happen between the vault and the roll. |

## `+BREACH` (300 pts)

Smash through a door, trapdoor or gate with a [slide](/parkour/moves/slide). `breach` has no
thresholds; it fires on the breach itself.

## `+HIGH PROFILE` (300 pts)

Pull off a loud or explosive move, like breaking a door, while at a high enough flow rank.

| `high_profile` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `minimum_rank` | `s` | The lowest [flow rank](/parkour/style-system) that can trigger it. Set it to `max` to require Flow State. |

## `+DEALMAKER` (350 pts)

Land a [roll](/parkour/moves/roll) on top of a villager's workstation or a loot container.

| `dealmaker` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `workstation_materials` | list | Blocks that count as a workstation. The defaults cover the villager job blocks; see the [full file](/parkour/reference/bonuses-yml). |
| `loot_materials` | list | Blocks that count as loot. Defaults are `CHEST`, `TRAPPED_CHEST` and `BARREL`. |

## `+REBOUND CLING` (350 pts)

[Tic tac](/parkour/moves/tic-tac) into a [wall cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling) without ever
touching the ground.

| `rebound_cling` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_window_ticks` | `40` | How long after the tic tac the cling still counts. |

## `+EFFICIENCY BUMP` (350 pts)

Get the perfect speed boost on two consecutive [quick vaults](/parkour/moves/quick-vault).

| `efficiency_bump` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `speed_match_threshold` | `1.1` | How much speed you must carry out of each vault relative to what you went in with. |
| `delay_ticks` | `2` | How long after each vault your speed is measured. |

## `+LONG WAY DOWN` (400 pts)

Hold one continuous [wall run](/parkour/moves/wall-run) over a long stretch of wall.

| `long_way_down` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_wallrun_distance` | `12.0` | How far the single wall run has to carry you, in blocks. |

## `+VENTED` (400 pts)

Pass through an iron trapdoor on a full-speed [slide](/parkour/moves/slide).

| `vented` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `min_speed` | `0.32` | The slide speed you need at the moment you pass through. |

## `+DOUBLE DOWN` (400 pts)

Land two perfectly-timed [rolls](/parkour/moves/roll) close together.

| `double_down` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_window_millis` | `10000` | How far apart the two rolls can be. |

## `+DEALER'S CHOICE` (450 pts)

[Slide](/parkour/moves/slide) into [wall run](/parkour/moves/wall-run) into
[tic tac](/parkour/moves/tic-tac).

| `dealers_choice` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_chain_time_millis` | `3000` | How long the whole three-move chain can take. |

## `+GHOST RUN` (500 pts)

String together a long sequence of moves without taking any damage.

| `ghost_run` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_moves` | `30` | How many moves the damage-free run needs. |

## `+SKYLINE` (500 pts)

Chain [wall runs](/parkour/moves/wall-run) together without touching the ground or using any move
other than a [wall jump](/parkour/moves/wall-jump) between them.

| `skyline` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_streak` | `3` | How many wall runs you need in the chain. |

## `+CHAOS, CHAOS!` (600 pts)

Trigger several *distinct* moves in quick succession.

| `chaos_chaos` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `required_distinct_moves` | `5` | How many different moves you need. Repeats don't count. |
| `window_millis` | `5000` | How long you have to fit them all in. |
