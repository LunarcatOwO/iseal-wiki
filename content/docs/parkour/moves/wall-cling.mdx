---
title: Wall Cling
description: Grab and hang on a wall, then leap off when you're ready.
---

The **Wall Cling** lets you grab a wall and hang there instead of falling. From a cling you can catch
your breath mid-combo and then push off with a [Wall Jump](/parkour/moves/wall-jump). It's the "pause
and reposition" tool of the move set.

## How to perform

1. Approach a **wall** while airborne and press toward it (within interaction range).
2. You latch on. While clinging you slowly drift downward.
3. Release by jumping off, or let the cling time out.

A cling can be ended early by admins or by the [`/parkour reset`](/parkour/commands) command if a
player ever gets stuck.

## Tuning (`moves.yml` → `wall_cling`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `interaction_range` | `1.5` | How close to a wall you must be to grab it. |
| `lateral_speed` | `0.18` | Sideways movement speed along the wall. ( currently unused ) |
| `gravity_drift` | `0.005` | Slow downward drift while clinging. |
| `max_cling_ticks` | `100` | Maximum cling duration, in ticks (5 seconds). |
| `corner_threshold` | `0.35` | Sensitivity for detecting and handling wall corners. ( currently unused ) |
| `wall_offset` | `0.9` | Distance the player is held from the wall face. |
| `initial_height_offset` | `0.6` | Vertical offset applied when the cling begins. |
| `cling_walk_speed` | `0.05` | Movement speed while walking during a cling. ( currently unused ) |
| `cooldown_ticks` | `60` | Cooldown after releasing, in ticks (3 seconds). |
| `bossbar` | Wall Cling / `GREEN` | Boss bar name, color and overlay used for this move's cooldown display. |

<Callout title="Related bonuses" type="info">
  Short, deliberate clings drive **+CHECKPOINT**, **+TOUCH AND GO**, **+SMOOTH TRANSITION** and
  **+REBOUND CLING**. Dropping from a cling into a fight feeds combat bonuses like **+CRITICAL DROP**
  and **+EMERGENCY MEETING**.
</Callout>
