---
title: Wall Cling
description: Grab a ledge and hang on it, then climb up, jump off or drop down.
---

The **Wall Cling** lets you grab the edge of a wall and hang there instead of falling. From a cling
you can pull yourself up with a [Climb Vault](/parkour/moves/climb-vault), launch off with a
[Wall Jump](/parkour/moves/wall-jump), or just let go.

## How to perform

1. Face a wall whose top edge is between your feet and your head, with open space above it.
2. **Right-click the side of the block** while not sneaking.
3. You grab on and hang there, slowly slipping down.

While clinging:

- Your **experience bar** shows how much grip you have left. After 5 seconds you let go.
- **Right-click** again to climb up onto the ledge.
- **Double-tap jump** to wall jump off.
- **Sneak** to let go and drop.

Getting knocked away from the wall (by a hit or an explosion) makes you lose your grip.

## Settings (`moves.yml` → `wall_cling`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `interaction_range` | `1.5` | How close you need to be to grab a wall, in blocks. |
| `gravity_drift` | `0.005` | How fast you slip down while clinging. |
| `max_cling_ticks` | `100` | How long you can hang on, in ticks (5 seconds). |
| `wall_offset` | `0.9` | How far from the wall you hang. |
| `initial_height_offset` | `0.6` | How high you hang relative to the ledge. |
| `correction_gain` | `0.2` | How firmly you're pulled back into place if you drift. |
| `max_correction_step` | `0.15` | Strongest pull back into place in one tick. |
| `hold_deadzone` | `0.02` | Drift smaller than this is ignored. |
| `break_distance` | `1.5` | How far you can be pushed from the wall before you lose your grip, in blocks. |
| `lateral_speed` | `0.18` | Not used in this version. |
| `corner_threshold` | `0.35` | Not used in this version. |
| `cling_walk_speed` | `0.05` | Not used in this version. |
| `cooldown` | `60` | Cooldown after letting go, in ticks (3 seconds). |
| `bossbar` | Wall Cling / `GREEN` | Name, color and style of this move's cooldown boss bar. |

<Callout title="Related bonuses" type="info">
  Short, deliberate clings drive **+CHECKPOINT**, **+TOUCH AND GO**, **+WALL TO WALL** and
  **+REBOUND CLING**. Dropping from a cling into a fight feeds **+CRITICAL DROP** and
  **+EMERGENCY MEETING**.
</Callout>
