---
title: Climb Vault
description: Mantle up and over ledges and obstacles that are too tall to simply jump.
---

The **Climb Vault** is a mantle: when you reach a ledge or obstacle that's too tall to jump onto, the
climb vault pulls you up and over it. It's the move that keeps a run flowing over uneven terrain.

## How to perform

1. Run or jump toward a **ledge / low obstacle** within interaction range.
2. The climb vault fires, lifting you **up and forward** onto the top.

Follow a climb vault immediately with a [Quick Vault](/parkour/moves/quick-vault) for the
**+CATAPULT** bonus, or drop into a [Roll](/parkour/moves/roll) on the far side.

## Tuning (`moves.yml` → `climb_vault`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `interaction_range` | `1.0` | Reach for detecting a climbable ledge. |
| `vault_up` | `0.5` | Upward velocity applied by the mantle. |
| `vault_forward` | `0.5` | Forward velocity applied by the mantle. |
| `max_ticks` | `10` | Maximum duration of the vault motion, in ticks. |
| `cooldown` | `50` | Cooldown after the vault, in ticks (2.5 seconds). |
| `bossbar` | (set your name) / `YELLOW` | Boss bar name, color, overlay. Flag `PLAY_BOSS_MUSIC` is enabled. |

<Callout title="Set the boss bar name" type="warn">
  In the default `moves.yml`, the climb vault's boss bar name is a placeholder (`<green>TODO`). Set
  it to something meaningful for your server. Its `PLAY_BOSS_MUSIC` flag also triggers boss music
  while active — remove that flag if you don't want the dramatic soundtrack.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Related bonuses" type="info">
  Climb vaults enable **+CATAPULT** (into a quick vault) and the combat bonus **+FROM ABOVE** (killing
  a mob while dropping out of a climb vault). See [Style System](/parkour/style-system).
</Callout>
