---
title: Climb Vault
description: Pull yourself up and over ledges that are too tall to jump onto.
---

The **Climb Vault** pulls you up onto a ledge that's too tall to jump onto. It keeps a run flowing
over uneven terrain and is the way up from a [Wall Cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling).

## How to perform

**From the ground:**

1. Stand right in front of a ledge (within 1 block).
2. **Right-click the block.**
3. You're lifted up and forward onto the top.

**From a wall cling:** **right-click** while clinging to pull yourself up.

Either way, you need two blocks of open space on top of the ledge to land in, and room above your
head. For low obstacles you don't need it at all: use the [Quick Vault](/parkour/moves/quick-vault)
instead and keep your speed.

Follow a climb vault straight into a [Quick Vault](/parkour/moves/quick-vault) for **+CATAPULT**, or
into a [Slide](/parkour/moves/slide) for **+SMOOTH TRANSITION**.

## Settings (`moves.yml` → `climb_vault`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `interaction_range` | `1.0` | How close you need to be to the ledge, in blocks. |
| `vault_up` | `0.5` | Upward strength of the vault. |
| `vault_forward` | `0.35` | Forward strength of the vault. |
| `max_ticks` | `10` | Longest the vault motion lasts, in ticks. |
| `cooldown` | `50` | Cooldown after the vault, in ticks (2.5 seconds). |
| `bossbar` | Climb Vault / `YELLOW` | Name, color and style of this move's cooldown boss bar. |

<Callout title="Related bonuses" type="info">
  Climb vaults set up **+CATAPULT**, **+SMOOTH TRANSITION** and the combat bonus **+FROM ABOVE**
  (a kill while dropping out of a climb vault). See [Style System](/parkour/style-system).
</Callout>
