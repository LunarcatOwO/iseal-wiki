---
title: Roll
description: Roll on landing to cut fall damage, and crush whatever you land on.
---

The **Roll** is your landing tool. Time a sneak just before you hit the ground and you tuck into a
roll that **cuts most of your fall damage** and carries your momentum forward. Land *on* a mob and
you can crush it. The roll is the single most important move for surviving big drops, and it's the
gateway to the game's highest-scoring bonuses.

## How to perform

1. While falling, **sneak** within `sneak_window_ticks` of landing.
2. The roll fires, reducing the fall damage and giving a small forward/upward boost out of the
   landing.

## Timing and damage reduction

How early you sneak decides how much damage you shed. A roll can cancel **at most 90%** of the fall
damage, so you always take at least a tenth of it.

Sneak within **1 tick** of landing and you get the full 90%. Every extra tick early cuts the
remaining reduction to **three quarters** of what it was, so the payoff falls away fast:

| Ticks early | Damage reduction | Cooldown used | Ticks |
| ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 0 or 1 (**perfect**) | 90% | `cooldown.perfect` | `10` (0.5s) |
| 2 | 67.5% | `cooldown.imperfect` | `60` (3s) |
| 3 | ~50.6% | `cooldown.imperfect` | `60` (3s) |
| 4 | ~38.0% | `cooldown.imperfect` | `60` (3s) |
| … | × 0.75 each tick | `cooldown.imperfect` | `60` (3s) |
| Outside the window (**miss**) | none, you take the fall in full | `cooldown.miss` | `200` (10s) |

The same falloff scales the boost out of the roll, so a sloppy roll costs you momentum as well as
health.

<Callout type="warn">
  Because a missed roll has a long cooldown, spamming sneak on the way down is punished. The roll
  rewards *timing*, not mashing.
</Callout>

## Tuning (`moves.yml` → `roll`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sneak_window_ticks` | `10` | Size of the input window (ticks) before landing to trigger a roll. |
| `cooldown.perfect` | `10` | Cooldown after a perfectly-timed roll. |
| `cooldown.imperfect` | `60` | Cooldown after an imperfect roll. |
| `cooldown.miss` | `200` | Cooldown after a missed roll. |
| `boost_y` | `0.2` | Upward boost coming out of the roll. |
| `boost_mult` | `0.5` | Multiplier on the forward momentum preserved, before the timing falloff. |
| `min_damage` | `1.0` | Fall damage below this doesn't count as a roll opportunity. |
| `crush_base_damage` | `4.0` | Base damage dealt to mobs you land on. |
| `crush_radius` | `1.75` | Radius of the landing crush effect. |
| `bossbar` | Roll / `WHITE` | Boss bar name, color and overlay used for this move's cooldown display. |
