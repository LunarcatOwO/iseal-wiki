title: Roll
description: Roll on landing to cancel fall damage — and crush whatever you land on.
---

The **Roll** is your landing tool. Time a sneak just before you hit the ground and you tuck into a
roll that **negates fall damage** and carries your momentum forward. Land *on* a mob and you can crush
it. The roll is the single most important move for surviving big drops — and it's the gateway to the
game's highest-scoring bonuses.

## How to perform

1. While falling, **sneak** within the timing window just before you land.
2. A well-timed roll cancels the fall damage and gives a small forward/upward boost out of the
   landing.

The quality of your timing determines the cooldown:

| Timing | Result | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Perfect** | Frame-perfect roll — no damage, full momentum. | `10` ticks (0.5s) |
| **Imperfect** | Roll landed, but slightly off. | `60` ticks (3s) |
| **Miss** | Missed the window — you take the fall normally. | `200` ticks (10s) |

<Callout type="warn">
  Because a missed roll has a long cooldown, spamming sneak on the way down is punished — the roll
  rewards *timing*, not mashing.
</Callout>

## Tuning (`moves.yml` → `roll`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sneak_window_ticks` | `10` | Size of the input window (ticks) before landing to trigger a roll. |
| `cooldown.perfect` | `10` | Cooldown after a perfectly-timed roll. |
| `cooldown.imperfect` | `60` | Cooldown after an imperfect roll. |
| `cooldown.miss` | `200` | Cooldown after a missed roll. |
| `boost_y` | `0.2` | Upward boost coming out of the roll. |
| `boost_mult` | `0.5` | Multiplier on the forward momentum preserved. |
| `min_damage` | `1.0` | Minimum fall damage threshold for a roll to matter. |
| `crush_base_damage` | `4.0` | Base damage dealt to mobs you land on. |
| `crush_radius` | `1.75` | Radius of the landing crush effect. |
| `bossbar` | Roll / `WHITE` | Boss bar name, color and overlay shown while active. |
