---
title: Roll
description: Roll on landing to cut fall damage, and crush whatever you land on.
---

The **Roll** is your landing move. Press sneak just before you hit the ground and you tuck into a
roll that **cuts most of your fall damage** and carries your speed forward. Land on a mob and you
crush it. The roll is the most important move for surviving big drops, and it's behind the highest
scoring bonuses in the game.

## How to perform

1. While falling, **press sneak** shortly before you land.
2. When you hit the ground you roll, taking less fall damage and getting a small boost out of the
   landing.

The roll only kicks in for falls that would hurt.

## Timing

The later you press sneak, the better the roll. A roll can cancel **at most 90%** of the fall damage,
so you always take at least a tenth of it.

Press sneak within **1 tick** of landing for the full 90%. Every tick earlier than that leaves you
with three quarters of the previous reduction, so the payoff falls away fast:

| Ticks early | Damage reduction | Cooldown used | Ticks |
| ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 0 or 1 (**perfect**) | 90% | `cooldown.perfect` | `10` (0.5s) |
| 2 | 67.5% | `cooldown.imperfect` | `60` (3s) |
| 3 | ~50.6% | `cooldown.imperfect` | `60` (3s) |
| 4 | ~38.0% | `cooldown.imperfect` | `60` (3s) |
| … | × 0.75 each tick | `cooldown.imperfect` | `60` (3s) |
| Too early (**miss**) | none, you take the full fall | `cooldown.miss` | `200` (10s) |

A sloppy roll also gives you a smaller boost out of the landing.

<Callout type="warn">
  A missed roll has a long cooldown, so mashing sneak on the way down is punished. The roll rewards
  *timing*.
</Callout>

## Settings (`moves.yml` → `roll`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sneak_window_ticks` | `10` | How early (in ticks) you can press sneak and still roll. |
| `cooldown.perfect` | `10` | Cooldown after a perfect roll. |
| `cooldown.imperfect` | `60` | Cooldown after an imperfect roll. |
| `cooldown.miss` | `200` | Cooldown after a missed roll. |
| `boost_y` | `0.2` | Upward boost out of the roll. |
| `boost_mult` | `0.5` | How much forward speed you keep out of the roll. |
| `min_damage` | `1.0` | Falls that would deal less damage than this don't trigger a roll. |
| `crush_base_damage` | `4.0` | Damage dealt to mobs you land on. |
| `crush_radius` | `1.75` | How far around you the landing crush reaches, in blocks. |
| `bossbar` | Roll / `WHITE` | Name, color and style of this move's cooldown boss bar. |

</Callout>
