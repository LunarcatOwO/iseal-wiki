---
title: Wall Jump
description: Kick off a wall during a wall run or cling to gain height and distance.
---

The **Wall Jump** launches you off a wall you're running on or clinging to. It's how you turn wall
contact into forward progress: bounce between two walls, or spring off one to clear a gap.

## How to perform

1. Be on a wall, either mid [Wall Run](/parkour/moves/wall-run) or in a
   [Wall Cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling), with an **empty main hand**.
2. **Double-tap jump.**
3. You launch up and away from the wall.

Aim where you want to go. If you're looking away from the wall you launch in the direction you're
looking; if you're looking along it, you launch straight out from it. You can't wall jump while
looking into the wall.

Alternate wall jumps between two close walls to climb a shaft, or jump straight into another wall run.

## Settings (`moves.yml` → `wall_jump`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_angle` | `120.0` | How far you can look into the wall (in degrees, measured from looking straight away from it) and still jump. `90` means you have to look at least along the wall. |
| `jump_y` | `0.45` | Upward strength of the jump. |
| `jump_multiplier` | `0.7` | Horizontal strength of the jump. |
| `particle_count` | `12` | Number of particles at the kick-off point. |
| `cooldown` | `60` | Cooldown after a wall jump, in ticks (3 seconds). |
| `bossbar` | Wall Jump / `GREEN` | Name, color and style of this move's cooldown boss bar. |

<Callout title="Related bonuses" type="info">
  Wall jumps set up **+SKYLINE**, **+CLOSE QUARTERS** and the mythic **+LEAP OF FAITH** (wall jump
  into a huge fall and land unhurt). See [Style System](/parkour/style-system).
</Callout>
