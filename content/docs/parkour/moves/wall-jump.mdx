---
title: Wall Jump
description: Kick off a wall to convert a wall run or cling into height and horizontal distance.
---

The **Wall Jump** launches you away from a wall you are running on or clinging to. It's how you turn
vertical contact into forward progress. Bounce between two walls, or spring off a surface to clear a
gap.

## How to perform

1. Be in contact with a wall, mid [Wall Run](/parkour/moves/wall-run) or
   [Wall Cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling).
2. **Jump.** You launch up and away from the wall, along the direction you're facing.

Out of a wall run you also have to be looking within `max_angle` of the wall; out of a cling you don't.

Alternate wall jumps between two close walls to climb a shaft, or jump straight into another wall run.

## Tuning (`moves.yml` → `wall_jump`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `max_angle` | `90.0` | Maximum angle (degrees) between your look direction and the wall for the jump to trigger. Only applies while you are in the **wall run** state. |
| `jump_y` | `0.45` | Upward velocity applied by the jump. |
| `jump_multiplier` | `0.7` | Multiplier on the horizontal push away from the wall. |
| `particle_count` | `12` | Number of particles spawned at the kick-off point. |
| `cooldown` | `60` | Cooldown after a wall jump, in ticks (3 seconds). |
| `bossbar` | Wall Jump / `GREEN` | Boss bar name, color and overlay used for this move's cooldown display. |

<Callout title="Related bonuses" type="info">
  Wall jumps set up chains for **+WALL TO WALL**, **+REBOUND CLING**, **+SKYLINE**, and the mythic
  **+LEAP OF FAITH** (wall jump into a huge fall, landing unhurt). See
  [Style System](/parkour/style-system).
</Callout>
