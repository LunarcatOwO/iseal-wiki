---
title: Tic Tac
description: Kick off a nearby wall in mid-air to redirect your momentum on the fly.
---

The **Tic Tac** is a mid-air redirect. Kick off a nearby wall while airborne to instantly change your
horizontal direction — dodge a projectile, snake around a corner, or set up the next wall contact. It
can also shove and damage mobs you push off of.

## How to perform

1. While **airborne**, be near a wall (within the max distance).
2. Trigger the tic tac — you push **off and away** from the wall, gaining a burst up, away, and
   forward.

Because it works off any nearby surface, the tic tac is the glue between other moves:
[Wall Run](/parkour/moves/wall-run) → tic tac → [Wall Cling](/parkour/moves/wall-cling), or
[Slide](/parkour/moves/slide) → [Wall Run](/parkour/moves/wall-run) → tic tac for
**+DEALER'S CHOICE**.

## Tuning (`moves.yml` → `tic_tac`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `upward_force` | `0.5` | Upward velocity applied by the kick. |
| `push_away_force` | `0.6` | Velocity pushing you away from the wall. |
| `forward_force` | `0.4` | Forward velocity applied in your travel direction. |
| `max_distance` | `2.0` | Maximum distance to a usable wall, in blocks. |
| `mob_push_multiplier` | `0.6` | Knockback multiplier when kicking off a mob. |
| `mob_impact_damage` | `2.0` | Damage dealt to a mob you tic-tac off of. |
| `cooldown` | `40` | Cooldown after a tic tac, in ticks (2 seconds). |
| `bossbar` | Tic Tac / `GREEN` | Boss bar name, color and overlay shown while active. |

<Callout title="Related bonuses" type="info">
  Tic tacs enable **+REBOUND CLING**, **+DEALER'S CHOICE**, and combat plays like **+HIT &amp; RUN**
  (killing with a tic tac) and **+BLUE HEART** (redirecting to dodge a projectile). See the
  [Style System](/parkour/style-system).
</Callout>
