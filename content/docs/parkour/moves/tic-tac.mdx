---
title: Tic Tac
description: Kick off a wall or a mob in mid-air to change direction on the fly.
---

The **Tic Tac** is a mid-air kick. Push off a nearby wall to change direction and gain a little
height: dodge a projectile, snake around a corner, or line up your next move. You can also kick off
hostile mobs, hurting them in the process.

## How to perform

**Off a wall:**

1. Jump toward a wall and get close to it (within 2 blocks).
2. **Left-click the side of a block** (not the top or bottom) while not sneaking.
3. You push off and away from the wall, up and in the direction you're looking.

**Off a mob:** while in the air, **right-click a hostile mob** close to you. You push off it like a
wall (a bit weaker) and deal it a small hit.

## Charges

Instead of a normal cooldown, the tic tac has **charges**:

- You can tic tac up to **3 times in a row**, with a short pause (0.25 seconds) between each.
- The chain ends when you use your last charge, or after **3 seconds** without another tic tac.
  Then you wait **4 seconds** before you can tic tac again.
- **Landing** refills your charges mid-chain, without the long wait.

## Settings (`moves.yml` → `tic_tac`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `upward_force` | `0.5` | Upward strength of the kick. |
| `push_away_force` | `0.6` | How strongly you're pushed away from the wall. |
| `forward_force` | `0.4` | How strongly you're pushed in the direction you're looking. |
| `max_distance` | `2.0` | How close the wall or mob has to be, in blocks. |
| `max_charges` | `3` | Tic tacs you can chain before the long cooldown. |
| `charge_cooldown` | `5` | Pause between tic tacs in a chain, in ticks. |
| `max_charge_window` | `60` | How long you have for the next tic tac before the chain ends, in ticks (3 seconds). |
| `long_cooldown` | `80` | Cooldown after a chain ends, in ticks (4 seconds). |
| `reset_on_ground` | `true` | Whether landing refills your charges. |
| `mob_push_multiplier` | `0.6` | How strong a kick off a mob is compared to a wall. |
| `mob_impact_damage` | `2.0` | Damage dealt to a mob you kick off. |
| `bossbar` | Tic Tac / `GREEN` | Name, color and style of this move's cooldown boss bar. |
