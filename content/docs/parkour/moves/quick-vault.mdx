The **Quick Vault** is a speed-preserving hurdle. Where the [Climb Vault](/parkour/moves/climb-vault)
mantles taller walls, the quick vault skims *over low obstacles* like fences, railings and single
blocks without slowing you down. Nail the timing and you keep (or even gain) momentum.

## How to perform

1. Approach a **low obstacle** at speed, on the ground, with an **empty main hand** and **not
   sneaking**.
2. **Left-click the obstacle**, either the block itself or the block directly above it, while you're
   within `interaction_range` of it.
3. You hop up and forward over the obstacle.

The vault's direction blends where you're **moving** and where you're **looking**, so aim through the
obstacle in the direction you want to exit.

## Tuning (`moves.yml` → `quick_vault`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `speed_threshold` | `0.05` | Minimum speed required to trigger the vault. |
| `interaction_range` | `3.0` | Reach for detecting a vaultable obstacle ahead. |
| `quick_vault_up` | `0.5` | Upward velocity applied by the vault. |
| `quick_vault_forward` | `0.45` | Forward velocity applied by the vault. |
| `dir_velocity_weight` | `0.7` | Weight of your current movement direction in the exit vector. |
| `dir_look_weight` | `0.3` | Weight of your look direction in the exit vector. |
| `cooldown` | `10` | Cooldown after the vault, in ticks (0.5 seconds). |
| `bossbar` | Quick Vault / `GREEN` | Boss bar name, color and overlay used for this move's cooldown display. |
