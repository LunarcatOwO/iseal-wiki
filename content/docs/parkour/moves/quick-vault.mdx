title: Quick Vault
description: Hop over low obstacles at speed without breaking stride.

The **Quick Vault** is a fast hurdle. Where the [Climb Vault](/parkour/moves/climb-vault) pulls you
up taller ledges, the quick vault skims over **low obstacles** like fences, railings and single blocks
without slowing you down. Time it well and you can even come out faster.

## How to perform

1. Run at a **low obstacle** on the ground, with an **empty main hand** and **not sneaking**.
2. **Left-click the obstacle** (the block itself or the block just above it) when you're close.
3. You hop up and forward over it.

The direction of the vault mixes where you're **moving** and where you're **looking**, so look
through the obstacle toward where you want to land.

## Settings (`moves.yml` → `quick_vault`)

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `speed_threshold` | `0.05` | Speed you need to vault. |
| `interaction_range` | `3.0` | How far ahead an obstacle can be, in blocks. |
| `quick_vault_up` | `0.5` | Upward strength of the vault. |
| `quick_vault_forward` | `0.45` | Forward strength of the vault. |
| `dir_velocity_weight` | `0.7` | How much your movement direction counts toward the vault direction. |
| `dir_look_weight` | `0.3` | How much your look direction counts toward the vault direction. |
| `cooldown` | `10` | Cooldown after the vault, in ticks (0.5 seconds). |
| `bossbar` | Quick Vault / `GREEN` | Name, color and style of this move's cooldown boss bar. |
