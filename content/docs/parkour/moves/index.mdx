Parkour Project has **eight moves**. Each one is useful on its own, but they're built to be chained
into one continuous route.

## Before you start

- Moves only work in **Survival** and **Adventure** mode.
- Several moves (wall jump, quick vault and others) need an **empty main hand**.
- Sneaking blocks most moves, except the ones that use sneak (slide and roll).

## Things every move shares

### Unlocked and enabled
Each move has two settings in `moves.yml`:

- **`enabled`** decides whether the move exists on the server at all.
- **`default_unlocked`** decides whether new players get the move automatically.

Admins can change this per player with `/parkour moves unlock|lock`, and players can switch their own
moves on and off with `/parkour moves enable|disable`. See [Commands](/parkour/commands).

### Cooldowns
Most moves have a **cooldown** (in ticks; 20 ticks = 1 second) before you can use them again. The
[Roll](/parkour/moves/roll) has three different cooldowns depending on how well you time it, and the
[Tic Tac](/parkour/moves/tic-tac) uses charges instead.

Players choose how cooldowns are shown with `/parkour ui cooldowns`: a **boss bar**, a line in the
**action bar**, or **chat** messages.

### Boss bars
Each move has its own boss bar name, color and style in `moves.yml`. The boss bar only shows the
move's **remaining cooldown**.

<Callout type="info">
  The numbers on the following pages are the **defaults**. Everything can be changed in
  `moves.yml`; see the [full reference](/parkour/reference/moves-yml).
</Callout>

### Moves feed the style system
Every move adds to your **combo**, and chaining moves in the right ways triggers style **bonuses**
that raise your flow score and rank. See [Style System](/parkour/style-system).
