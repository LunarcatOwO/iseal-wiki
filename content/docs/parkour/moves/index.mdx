Parkour Project ships with **eight movement abilities**. Every move is physics-driven rather than
scripted, with no teleporting, and moves *combine* into flowing routes.

## Shared concepts

### Enabled, unlocked & default state
Every move has two independent switches in `moves.yml`:

- **`enabled`** controls whether the move exists on the server at all.
- **`default_unlocked`** controls whether players have it unlocked automatically when they first join.

Admins can override these per player with `/parkour moves unlock|lock|enable|disable`. See
[Commands](/parkour/commands).

### Cooldowns
Most moves have a **`cooldown`** (in ticks; 20 ticks = 1 second) to prevent spamming. Remaining
cooldowns can be shown through one or more **cooldown UIs** (`BOSSBAR`, `CHAT`, `TOOLBAR`) that each
player can toggle. The [Roll](/parkour/moves/roll) is special, with *three* cooldowns depending on
how well you time it.

### Boss bars
Each move defines its own **boss bar** (name, color, overlay) in `moves.yml`. This is what the
`BOSSBAR` cooldown UI draws: the bar tracks that move's **remaining cooldown** and nothing else. It
isn't an indicator that the move is currently happening.

<Callout type="info">
  All the numeric values on the following pages are the **defaults**. Everything is tunable in
  `moves.yml`; see the [full reference](/parkour/reference/moves-yml) for the complete file.
</Callout>

### Moves feed the style system
Chaining moves builds your **combo**, which multiplies what every style **bonus** pays out. The
bonuses are what raise your flow score and your flow rank. That whole layer is documented under
[Style System](/parkour/style-system).
