---
title: The Gems and Their Abilities
description: Overview of every gem and what each of its abilities does.
---

Every gem has three active abilities, used by clicking with the gem in your hand, plus a **Passive**
effect:

- **Right Click** and **Left Click** each trigger an ability.
- **Shift Click** triggers the third ability: hold **sneak** and press either mouse button.
- The **Passive** is a potion effect you get while the gem is in your inventory. By default it
  starts once the gem is **above level 3** (set by `unlockNewAbilitiesOnLevelX`).

Each ability has its own cooldown, which gets shorter as the gem levels up. Higher levels also make
most abilities stronger. The descriptions below are the defaults; server owners can change the
passive effects and cooldowns.

## Strength Gem

<GemArt gem="strengthgem" name="Strength Gem" />

| Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Right Click** | Gives you Strength and Saturation. |
| **Left Click** | A shockwave that knocks nearby players away, damages them and gives them Weakness. |
| **Shift Click** | Creates an arena around you that nobody else can enter, and gives you Strength. Useful for healing up. |
| **Passive** | Haste. |

## Healing Gem

<GemArt gem="healgem" name="Healing Gem" />

| Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Right Click** | Parry: blocks almost all damage for a split second. |
| **Left Click** | Gives you Regeneration. |
| **Shift Click** | Instantly heals you and gives you Saturation. |
| **Passive** | Regeneration. |

## Air Gem

<GemArt gem="airgem" name="Air Gem" />

| Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Right Click** | Creates a tether of wind to the player you're looking at, pulling them toward you, damaging and slowing them. |
| **Left Click** | A burst of wind that launches everything nearby into the air and damages players. |
| **Shift Click** | Creates a cloud of smoke, makes you invisible for a few seconds and propels you forward. Also works with elytras. |
| **Passive** | Speed. |

## Fire Gem

<GemArt gem="firegem" name="Fire Gem" />

| Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- |

| **Left Click** | Creates an explosion around you that damages nearby entities, while protecting you from the blast. |
