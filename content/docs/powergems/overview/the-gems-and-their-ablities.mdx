description: Overview of every gem and what each of its abilities does.
---

Every gem has four abilities: a **Right Click**, a **Left Click**, a **Shift Click**, and a **Passive**
effect that is always active while the gem is held.

<Callout title="How to use Shift Click abilities" type="idea">
  A gem's **Shift Click** ability is used by holding **sneak** and pressing either mouse button.
</Callout>

## Strength Gem

<GemArt gem="strengthgem" name="Strength Gem" />

| Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Right Click** | Provides Saturation, Strength and Resistance potion effects. |
| **Left Click** | A shockwave that sends nearby entities flying and damages them. |
| **Shift Click** | Creates an area around the user that prevents anyone from entering it. |
| **Passive** | Haste. |

## Healing Gem

<GemArt gem="healgem" name="Healing Gem" />

| Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Right Click** | Parry attacks. |
| **Left Click** | Slowly regenerate with 1 minute of Regeneration II. |
| **Shift Click** | Instantly heals the user. |
| **Passive** | Regeneration. |

## Air Gem

<GemArt gem="airgem" name="Air Gem" />

| Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Right Click** | Creates a wind that pulls the target toward the user. |
| **Left Click** | Unleashes a burst of wind, launching everything nearby into the air. |
| **Shift Click** | Creates a smoke cloud, granting temporary invisibility and propelling the user forward. Also works with elytras. |
| **Passive** | Slow Falling. |

## Fire Gem

<GemArt gem="firegem" name="Fire Gem" />

| Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Right Click** | Creates a fiery aura around the user, granting temporary fire resistance and setting nearby blocks on fire. |
| **Left Click** | Launches a fireball after a charge-up in the direction faced, causing a powerful explosion on impact. |
| **Shift Click** | Creates an explosion around the user, damaging nearby entities and setting them on fire. |
| **Passive** | Fire Resistance. |

## Iron Gem

<GemArt gem="irongem" name="Iron Gem" />

| Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- |

## Lightning Gem

<GemArt gem="lightgem" name="Lightning Gem" />

| Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Right Click** | Strikes lightning at the aimed position and any nearby entities, damaging them. |
| **Left Click** | Launches the user forward in the direction they are looking. Also works with elytras. |
| **Shift Click** | Emits a thunder sound and applies Glowing to nearby entities, excluding the user. |
| **Passive** | Speed. |
