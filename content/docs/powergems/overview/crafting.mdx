The following recipes are the defaults and can be customized by server owners. For details on changing them, see [Configuration](/powergems/fundamentals/getting-set-up/configuration).

## Crafting a New Gem

Follow the default recipe shown below (or your customized variant in `recipes.yml`).

	<figcaption>Default New Gem Recipe. Update this image if you customize recipes.</figcaption>

<Callout type="idea">
  If you change the shape or materials in `recipes.yml`, share the updated recipe with your players
  (for example, by replacing the image above) so they know how to craft it.
</Callout>

## Upgrading Gems

Gems can be upgraded using a universal pattern by level. The exact materials per level are configurable.

<Callout type="warn">
  The Emerald in the middle is the gem you are upgrading, not an emerald.
</Callout>

<figure>
	<img src="/images/gem_upgrade.png" alt="Gem Upgrade recipe diagram (Default)" />
	<figcaption>Gem Upgrade Recipe (Default). Adjust based on your server’s settings. The emerald in the middle is a gem</figcaption>
</figure>

Hint: Base cooldowns decrease with levels (see `cooldown.yml`) and `cooldownBoostPerLevelInSeconds`.
