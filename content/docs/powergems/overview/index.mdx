---
title: Overview
description: The gems, what they do, and how to craft and upgrade them.
---

Gems are special items that give their holder abilities. There are ten gems, each with a Right
Click, Left Click and Shift Click ability plus a passive effect. Gems have **levels**: higher levels
mean stronger abilities and shorter cooldowns, and by default the passive effect starts above level 3.

New players get a random gem on their first join (`giveGemOnFirstLogin`). More can be crafted, and
existing gems can be upgraded with a crafting recipe or, if the server enables it, by getting kills.
