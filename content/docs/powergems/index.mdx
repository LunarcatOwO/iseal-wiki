Welcome to the **PowerGems** wiki!

PowerGems adds **gems**: special items that give players powerful abilities. By default every player
gets a random gem the first time they join. Each of the ten gems has three active abilities and a
passive effect, and gems can be **crafted** and **upgraded** to make them stronger.

<Callout type="info">
  PowerGems needs **SealLib** and runs on **Paper 1.20.1+**. **WorldGuard** and **CombatLogX** are
  supported if you have them.
</Callout>
