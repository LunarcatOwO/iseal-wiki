---
title: Frequently Asked Questions
description: Common questions about PowerGems and quick answers.
---

### What server software do I need?

A Paper server on **1.20.1** or newer, with **SealLib** installed. See
[Quick Start](/powergems/quick-start).

### Where can I get the resource packs?

On [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/collection/wGK8f7HI)!

### I edited the config but nothing changed. Why?

`/reloadconfig` (`/rc`) doesn't reliably apply config changes. After editing `config.yml` or any file
in the `config` folder, **fully restart your server**.

### `/givegem fire` says the gem is invalid. Why?

Gem names are case sensitive: use `/givegem Fire`. Tab completion shows the right names.

### My gem's passive effect isn't working. Why?

By default the passive effect only starts once the gem is **above level 3**. Upgrade the gem, or
lower `unlockNewAbilitiesOnLevelX` in [`config.yml`](/powergems/fundamentals/getting-set-up/configuration/general-config).

### What are the default recipes?

See the [Crafting](/powergems/overview/crafting) page. Server owners can change them in `recipes.yml`.

### Can I turn off some gems?

Yes. Set the gem to `false` in `activeGems.yml`. See
[Advanced Configs](/powergems/fundamentals/getting-set-up/configuration/advanced-configs).

### Can I block gems in certain areas?

Yes, with WorldGuard. Keep `isWorldGuardSupportEnabled` on and block gem use in the regions you want.

### Is there a Bedrock edition pack?

Not officially. Community-made packs may be shared on [Discord](https://discord.iseal.dev); use them
at your own discretion.

### Where can I see what every gem does?

See [The Gems and Their Abilities](/powergems/overview/the-gems-and-their-ablities).

### How can I add my own gems?

With the API. See [API Usage](/powergems/api-usage).

### Is there a single "best" gem?

The gems are meant to be balanced against each other, and every cooldown and passive effect can be
tuned in the config files to suit your server.

### Where can I get help?

Join the community [Discord](https://discord.iseal.dev).
